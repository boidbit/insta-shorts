# Recorded sound effects (CC0)

These sounds replace the studio's synthesized ones of the same name (`studio/audio.py` uses `assets/sfx/<name>.ogg`
when it is here, else it makes the old sound itself). All are **CC0 1.0** (public domain): free for commercial use,
no attribution required. Picked from the CC0 part of the sound library of
[kaomei/stickman-video-director](https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director) (`b-audio-library`, MIT repo;
recordings keep their own licences). Changes: trimmed, peak-normalized, mono, OGG Vorbis; `footsteps` and `knock` are
several recordings in a row; `applause`, `crowd_cheer` and `thunder` are 4-second excerpts with a fade-out.
