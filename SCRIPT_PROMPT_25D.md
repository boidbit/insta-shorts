# 2.5D style for THIS video

This video uses the studio's **2.5D look**: every place is drawn as layered depth (the sky, the far buildings, trees, mountains or sea and the view through a window move slower than the floor when the camera moves, far things are softly blurred, windows and lamps glow, a blurred plant or table sits right in front of the lens). The people get light and shadow from the place. The script format and every allowed word stay exactly the same.

- Put `STYLE: 2.5d` as the second line, right after `HOOK:`.
- Any place works. These are the premium hand-made 2.5D sets with the most depth and life, prefer them when the story fits:
  - `kitchen`: a sunny daytime kitchen. Window with a view outside, sunlight with the window-grille shadow on the floor, a pressure cooker that steams, fridge, chimney. Good for mummy/beta, cooking, food, tiffin, "kaam pakda diya" jokes.
  - `gully` / `chai_tapri` / `kirana`: a lane at golden hour (sunset at the far end) with a kirana store, an electric pole with a tangle of wires, bunting and a chai stall with a counter. Good for dost, udhaar, chai, padosi, gossip, cricket-in-the-gully jokes.
  - `terrace`: a rooftop on a festival night (Diwali): fairy lights, diyas, a paper lantern, fireworks in the sky. Good for Diwali, crackers, family-on-the-roof, "naya saal", "shaadi ki raat" jokes. It is always night here: do not add a time word.
  - `room` / `living_room`: the family's living room by day: sofa under a window with a view outside, a TV that flickers, a ceiling fan that turns, a rug, sunlight with dust in the beam, a family photo. Good for papa/mummy/beta, TV remote fights, results day, rishta, guests, "padhai kar lo" jokes.
  - `school` / `classroom`: a school classroom by day: blackboard with chalk writing, teacher's table with a globe, benches, two ceiling fans, a window onto the school ground with chalk dust in the sunlight. Good for teacher/madam and students, homework, exams, backbenchers, "kal test hai" jokes.
- The other places (street, office, jungle, beach, mandap, hospital...) get their depth automatically. Only `plain` and `night_road` stay flat.
- A time word after the place still works (`SCENE: kitchen night`, `SCENE: gully rain`): the set is re-lit, the lamps keep glowing.
- Depth shows best when the camera and the people move through it: use `CUT` (a new shot), `near` / `far` (`B -> near shock`) so people step towards or away from the camera, and for an arrival `NAME -> enter back` (they come from the far corner of the place towards the camera, small to full size: the strongest entrance in 2.5D). The camera follows whoever moves, so one such move per video is enough.
- Keep it to 1-2 places: one strong 2.5D place reads better than many quick ones.
