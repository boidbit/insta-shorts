# 2.5D style for THIS video

This video uses the studio's **2.5D look**: some places are drawn as layered sets with real depth (the far end of the lane, the sky and the window view move slower than the floor when the camera moves, far things are softly blurred, lights glow, blurred things pass right in front of the lens). The people, the script format and every allowed word stay exactly the same.

- Put `STYLE: 2.5d` as the second line, right after `HOOK:`.
- Set the story in these 2.5D places (other places still work, but look flat like the normal style):
  - `kitchen`: a sunny daytime kitchen. Window with a view outside, sunlight with the window-grille shadow on the floor, a pressure cooker that steams, fridge, chimney. Good for mummy/beta, cooking, food, tiffin, "kaam pakda diya" jokes.
  - `gully` / `chai_tapri` / `kirana`: a lane at golden hour (sunset at the far end) with a kirana store, an electric pole with a tangle of wires, bunting and a chai stall with a counter. Good for dost, udhaar, chai, padosi, gossip, cricket-in-the-gully jokes.
  - `terrace`: a rooftop on a festival night (Diwali): fairy lights, diyas, a paper lantern, fireworks in the sky. Good for Diwali, crackers, family-on-the-roof, "naya saal", "shaadi ki raat" jokes. It is always night here: do not add a time word.
- A time word after the place still works (`SCENE: kitchen night`, `SCENE: gully rain`): the set is re-lit, the lamps keep glowing.
- Depth shows best when the camera moves: use `CUT` (a new shot), and `near` / `far` (`B -> near shock`) so people step towards or away from the camera.
- Keep it to 1-2 places: one strong 2.5D place reads better than many quick ones.
