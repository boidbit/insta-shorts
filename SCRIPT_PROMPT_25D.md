# 2.5D style for THIS video

This video uses the studio's **2.5D look**: every place is drawn as layered depth (the sky, the far buildings, trees, mountains or sea and the view through a window move slower than the floor when the camera moves, far things are softly blurred, windows and lamps glow, a blurred plant or table sits right in front of the lens). The people get light and shadow from the place. The script format and every allowed word stay exactly the same.

- Put `STYLE: 2.5d` as the second line, right after `HOOK:`.
- Any place works. These are the premium hand-made 2.5D sets with the most depth and life, prefer them when the story fits:
  - `kitchen`: a sunny daytime kitchen. Window with a view outside, sunlight with the window-grille shadow on the floor, a pressure cooker that steams, fridge, chimney. Good for mummy/beta, cooking, food, tiffin, "kaam pakda diya" jokes.
  - `gully` / `chai_tapri` / `kirana`: a lane at golden hour (sunset at the far end) with a kirana store, an electric pole with a tangle of wires, bunting and a chai stall with a counter. Good for dost, udhaar, chai, padosi, gossip, cricket-in-the-gully jokes.
  - `terrace`: a rooftop on a festival night (Diwali): fairy lights, diyas, a paper lantern, fireworks in the sky. Good for Diwali, crackers, family-on-the-roof, "naya saal", "shaadi ki raat" jokes. It is always night here: do not add a time word.
  - `room` / `living_room`: the family's living room by day: sofa under a window with a view outside, a TV that flickers, a ceiling fan that turns, a rug, sunlight with dust in the beam, a family photo. Good for papa/mummy/beta, TV remote fights, results day, rishta, guests, "padhai kar lo" jokes.
  - `school` / `classroom`: a school classroom by day: blackboard with chalk writing, teacher's table with a globe, benches, two ceiling fans, a window onto the school ground with chalk dust in the sunlight. Good for teacher/madam and students, homework, exams, backbenchers, "kal test hai" jokes.
  - `bedroom`: a bedroom by day: the bed with pillows and a quilt, a bedside lamp, a wardrobe, a window with curtains, a ceiling fan. Good for waking up late, phone in bed, "so ja", exam-night jokes.
  - `office` / `meeting_room`: an office by day with a glass window onto the city, desks with monitors, a water cooler, the TEAM AWESOME board; a meeting room with a glass wall, a TV with this quarter's targets and a long table. Good for boss, HR, appraisal, Gen-Z employee, "family", deadline jokes.
  - `jungle`: a jungle by day in three depths (misty trunks, vines, big trees, ferns, a path), sun rays and falling leaves. Good for camping, getting lost, animals talking, survival jokes (`SCENE: jungle night fireflies` for night).
  - `street` / `bus_stop`: a busy shop street by day (MEDICAL, MOBILE, SWEETS, TAILOR, an electric pole with its wires, a zebra crossing) and a bus stop with its shelter, bench and a big tree; cars, autos and bikes drive by behind the people. Good for traffic, auto-wala, road-side, waiting-for-the-bus, "bhaiya kitna hua" jokes.
  - `market`: a sabzi bazaar with three stalls (SABZI, FRUITS, MASALA) and bunting; a `sabziwala` stands behind his stall's counter by himself. Good for bargaining, mehngai, aunty-vs-sabziwala jokes.
  - `festival_street`: a lit-up street at night: strings of lights, paper lanterns, a glowing MITHAI shop, a balloon seller, fireworks in the sky. It is always night here. Good for festival shopping, relatives, sweets, "bonus kab milega" jokes.
  - `railway`: a station platform, looking across the tracks at the DESIPUR JN board; a train rushes through now and then. Good for late trains, TT, coolie, "seat mili?" jokes.
  - `gaon`: a village by day: a mud house with a tiled roof and chulha smoke, a hand pump, a haystack, a neem tree, hens pecking, fields and hills behind. Good for city-vs-village, nani-ghar, NRI-returns jokes.
  - `hospital` / `bank` / `police_station` / `court` / `canteen`: a hospital lobby (OPD doors, a heart monitor that beats, the RECEPTION desk), a bank hall (CASH / LOANS / ENQUIRY glass windows, a TOKEN board that counts up), a police station (WANTED posters, the lock-up, the duty desk), a court room (the judge's raised bench, the witness box, the munshi's desk) and a college canteen (the chalk MENU, a counter of samosas, a steaming tea urn). The `receptionist`, `cashier`, `police`, `judge` / `gawah` / `munshi` and the canteen's `chaiwala` stand behind their counter by themselves. Good for queue, sarkari-kaam, "server down", court-drama and canteen-udhaar jokes.
  - `dhaba`: a highway dhaba at sunset: the lit SHER-E-DHABA board, strings of bulbs, a glowing tandoor, a charpai, a decorated truck behind. Good for road-trip, truck-driver, "dal makhani" jokes.
  - `mithai_shop` / `mobile_shop` / `medical_store` / `salon`: a sweet shop (MITHAI BHANDAR, a glass counter of laddoos and jalebi, a kadhai steaming, a fly about), a mobile shop (a wall of phones whose screens change, 5G SALE, EMI 0%), a chemist (MEDICAL STORE with a blinking green cross, the INSULIN fridge) and a salon (a mirror with bulbs, the red barber chair, a turning barber pole). Good for "fresh hai na?", EMI, "bina parchi dawai", "thoda sa kaatna" jokes.
  - `mall` / `cinema`: a mall with a glass roof, two floors of shops (SALE 50%, SHOES, GADGETS), shoppers upstairs and a moving escalator; a cinema hall with the film playing on the big screen, the projector's beam and rows of seats with heads. Good for sale-shopping, "bill kaun dega", phone-in-the-cinema, spoiler jokes.
  - `haunted_house`: the old house at night: a big moon, bats, a window whose light flickers, a lantern, fog over the ground. It is always night here. Good for bhoot, dare, cheap-flat, horror-comedy jokes.
- The other places (street, market, beach, hospital, bank...) get their depth automatically. Only `plain` and `night_road` stay flat.
- A time word after the place still works (`SCENE: kitchen night`, `SCENE: gully rain`): the set is re-lit, the lamps keep glowing.
- Depth shows best when the people move through it: `near` / `far` (`B -> near shock`: a step towards or away from the camera), a `CUT wide` now and then, and for an arrival `NAME -> enter back` (they come from the far corner of the place towards the camera, small to full size: the strongest entrance in 2.5D). The camera follows whoever moves, so one such move per video is enough; the comedy and the natural talk come first.
- Keep it to 1-2 places: one strong 2.5D place reads better than many quick ones.
