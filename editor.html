📝 Script (paste karo ya studio se aayi)

    Upar script daalo, ya studio page pe "🎬 Harkat editor" dabao.

    Kirdaar ko ungli se khiskao: is line ke baad wo wahan chal ke jaayega.

    Gol nishaan (haath, pair, sir) ko kheencho. Ye pose is line ke baad lagega, aur video me haath-pair dheere-dheere yahan aayenge.

    Saaman chuno, phir upar stage pe kheench ke rakho. Ye poori video me wahin rahega.
